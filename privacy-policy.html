BloodDirectory Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

BloodDirectory is created by Albin K P. This privacy policy explains how BloodDirectory collects, uses, stores, and shares information.

Information we collect

Visitors can browse the donor directory without creating an account. Donors who create an account provide an email address and password for authentication. Donor records may include the donor's name, phone number, blood group, location, and last donation date.

How information is used

The information is used to provide the donor directory, help people find and contact suitable donors, allow donors to maintain their donation date, and allow administrators to manage donor records.

Public donor information

Donor name, phone number, blood group, location, last donation date, and eligibility status are displayed publicly in the BloodDirectory donor directory. Donors should only submit information they agree to make public.

Service providers

BloodDirectory uses Supabase to provide authentication and database services. Information is processed and stored through Supabase according to its privacy and security practices. BloodDirectory does not sell user information and does not use donor information for advertising.

Security

BloodDirectory uses authenticated access and database access policies to restrict donor creation, donation-date updates, and administrative actions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Donor records are retained while they are needed for the directory. Administrators can delete donor records. To request deletion of an account and its associated donor data, use the BloodDirectory data deletion request page.

Children

BloodDirectory is not intended for children to create donor accounts or submit personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when BloodDirectory features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Albin K P through the BloodDirectory creator page.
